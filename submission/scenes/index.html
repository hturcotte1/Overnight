Oct 10 to 11: the whole loop, for real

  • Oct 10, morning

    An app idea was said out loud near the watch. Kiro spotted it, the wrist was asked, and it was ticked on the phone.

  • Oct 11, 2:00 AM

    The build job started by itself and passed the nightly sandbox check. Kiro Crew built “Plant Watering Tracker” in 2 min 40 s, for 4.59 Kiro credits.

  • 7:30 AM

  • 7:32 AM

Guardrails

An AI writes the code. These rules keep it in its place.

  • Nothing is built until the wearer ticks it.

  • What was heard is stored only on this Mac.Kiro reads it to spot ideas, with Kiro's content sharing switched off.

  • The builder only gets a short brief (title and pitch), never the words that were said.

  • Kiro Crew runs inside Overnight's own macOS sandbox. A check every night proves the GitHub login, the Bee login and the transcripts are out of its reach.

  • Publishing is done by Overnight, never by the agent.

  • A monthly cap on Kiro credits.
